Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gold Rate Today mobile application ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App.

Gold Rate Today provides indicative gold prices, historical price information, purity-based gold rates, and jewellery value calculation tools.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Gold Rate Today does not currently require users to create an account, sign in, or directly provide personal information such as their name, phone number, postal address, or email address.

However, certain technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the App.

Technical and Device Information

Depending on the services enabled in the App, technical information may include:

  • Device type and operating system information
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Approximate location inferred from network information
  • Ad interaction and performance information
  • Diagnostic or error information

Such information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google when advertising functionality is enabled.

3. Gold Price and Exchange Rate Data

The App retrieves market information from external data providers in order to calculate and display indicative gold prices.

Current and historical gold-market information may be obtained from Gold-API.

Foreign-exchange information, including USD/INR reference rates, may be obtained from Frankfurter.

These services may receive technical information such as your device's IP address when network requests are made.

Gold Rate Today does not control the privacy practices of these third-party providers. Users should review the respective privacy policies and terms of those services for additional information.

4. Local Storage and Cached Data

To improve performance and allow the App to continue displaying previously available information when an internet connection is temporarily unavailable, the App may store certain information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Previously retrieved gold rates
  • USD/INR exchange rates
  • Historical gold-price information
  • Last successful update time

This information is stored locally for application functionality and is not used to identify you personally.

5. Gold Calculator

The App includes a gold and jewellery value calculator. Users may enter values such as:

  • Gold weight
  • Gold purity or karat
  • Making charges
  • GST preferences

These calculator inputs are used to perform calculations within the App. We do not currently transmit these calculator values to our own servers.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

Gold Rate Today may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information in order to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, subject to applicable law and user consent.

Information processed for advertising purposes may include advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, ad interactions, and other technical data.

Depending on your location and applicable regulations, the App may request your consent before personalized advertising or certain types of data processing occur.

Users in regions such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a privacy or consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Where available, users may be given options relating to personalized and non-personalized advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Gold Rate Today may use third-party services including, but not necessarily limited to:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Gold-API
  • Frankfurter exchange-rate services
  • Google Play services

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App or its service providers may be used to:

  • Retrieve and display gold-market information
  • Convert international gold prices into Indian Rupees
  • Provide historical gold-price trends
  • Improve application reliability and performance

  • Detect technical problems
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers when required to provide the App's functionality, including advertising, market data, network services, and application distribution.

13. Gold Price Disclaimer

Gold prices displayed in Gold Rate Today are indicative, market-derived estimates.

They are not necessarily IBJA benchmark prices and should not be considered the final retail selling price offered by any particular jeweller.

Actual jewellery prices may differ because of factors including:

  • GST
  • Making charges
  • Wastage charges
  • Jeweller margins or premiums
  • Local market conditions
  • Taxes, duties, or other charges

The App and its calculator are intended for informational and estimation purposes only and do not constitute financial, investment, trading, tax, or legal advice.
